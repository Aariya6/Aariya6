01 / ABOUT THE THREAD

Technology is most interesting where it meets people.

I’m an AI engineer and product developer drawn to the space between rigorous systems and human questions. I like making complicated ideas tangible: a research record with a memory, a workflow that knows when to stop, a studio that leaves the music in your hands.

My work values legible sources, honest uncertainty, and interfaces that invite curiosity instead of demanding trust.

More work on GitHub ↗
How I approach the work +

Make the source visible. Name uncertainty. Keep important decisions with the person using the tool.

03 / PRACTICE TOOLS, NOT TROPHIES

The work is the experience.

My public projects are independent product builds, not a proxy for an unlisted employment history.

Across them, I’ve worked through source provenance and data modeling, local-first workflows, model-backed analysis, browser interactions, and creative interfaces. I keep the decisions close to the product: distinguish a signal from a fact, make automation pause when judgment matters, and let a person inspect or change the result.

04 / OPEN QUESTIONS RESEARCH & INTERESTS

A little more humanities.

Literature, philosophy, and the humanities keep my engineering questions honest. I’m drawn to how people interpret a text, how evidence accumulates, and where a tool should stop speaking for someone.

That thread runs through my experiments in digital humanities, trustworthy AI, environmental data, and creative technology.

Questions I keep returning to +

How can software help someone notice a connection without deciding what it means? What does responsible automation leave in human hands? How can a creative interface make room for play?
